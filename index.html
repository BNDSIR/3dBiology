Interactive MBBS 3D Dissector — Help & Voice Tutorial

Click any feature card or press "Play Full Voice Guide" to hear spoken instructions in English or বাংলা

1. 3D Navigation, Tap & Isolate Zoom

Rotate 360° with 1 finger or left-click drag. Pinch or scroll wheel to zoom. Single-tap any organ/bone to view its 5 MBBS Viva sections & live Wikipedia article. Double-tap any part to isolate it alone in 3D space.

2. Surgical Scalpel & Hover Voice Feedback

Click 🔪 Scalpel: ON on the bottom bar. As you hover over the 3D body, it highlights the target structure, shows its name, and speaks the anatomical name aloud if 🗣️ Hover Voice: ON is active. Click any superficial layer to dissect/peel it away with a surgical sound effect!

3. 3D Studio Lighting, MRI Slicer & Explode

Open ☰ Show Tools on the top-left to adjust 💡 3D Surgical Lighting brightness (50%–220%) and presets, slice through organs in Sagittal/Axial/Coronal planes with the 🩻 MRI Slicer, or separate layers radially with the 💥 Explode Slider.

4. Bilingual (EN ⇄ বাংলা) & Maximized Wikipedia

Click 🌐 EN | বাংলা to switch all clinical notes, voice narration, and Wikipedia lookups between English and Bengali. Click 🗖 Maximize on the Wikipedia card to open a full-screen textbook reading overlay.

5. MBBS Practical Spotter Viva Exam Mode

Click 🎯 Spotter Viva on the bottom bar to test yourself! The examiner will ask you to locate and tap specific bones, nerves, vessels, or organs on the 3D model and grade your score with clinical explanations.

6. Auto-Loading Separate Blender .GLB Files

Each tab automatically loads its matching Blender file from your folder (skeleton.glb, visceral.glb, nervous.glb, sense.glb, lymphatic.glb, digestive.glb, renal.glb, cardiovascular.glb), or you can drag & drop any .glb directly onto the page!
